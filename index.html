FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is MightyMe? How is it different from Mighty Minds?+

MightyMe is a cloud-based learning management system that delivers automated, cost-effective learning and teaching tools for learners and tutors. Mighty Minds is the scholarship program by MightyMe. It finds the mighty quotient in young minds through a series of scientific brain games.

How does MightyMe help with academics?+

MightyMe offers board-specific and subject-specific practice test packs made by subject experts. Questions come in four levels — Basic, Intermediate, Competitive and Challenger — to assess each student's skills.

Who are the users of MightyMe?+

Teachers, primary and secondary school students and their parents — and now whole schools, with MightyMe for Schools.

How can I become a Mighty Warrior?+

Students with the highest Mighty Points — earned from practice tests, finishing tasks on time and posting positive Mighty Deeds — become Mighty Warriors. Their profiles appear on our leaderboard.

Which test packs are available?+

Science, Social Science and Mathematics for CBSE and ICSE classes, in four levels: Basic, Intermediate, Competitive and Challenger.

How long does it take to set up MightyMe in a school?+

Usually 2–3 weeks. We help move your data and train your teachers, and attendance and parent messages work from the first week.
